# Add Regime Transition Event

## Why

Regime already changes only after three matching readable proposals, but Observatory does not yet
record that settled State change as a dated Event. The Event must preserve the same causal transition
mechanics without turning initial Regime availability into a change or duplicating Regime evidence.

## What Changes

Add pure, session-aligned detection of Regime Transition Events to the technical-analysis module.
Every settled change between different readable Regime labels is recorded once on its third
confirming readable session. The initial adoption of a readable Regime is not an Event.

The Event carries the prior and replacement Regime plus the three actual readable session dates that
confirmed the transition. Close, EMA50, EMA200, ATR14, Structure Break composition, Situations,
Opportunities, Observations, notifications, persistence, wording, scores, generalized detector
frameworks, and presentation-time recalculation remain outside this capability.
